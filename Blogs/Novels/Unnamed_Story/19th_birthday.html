Chapter 1105 - Memorable Birthday

Today is my 19th birthday.

For every birthday before this, since none of my friends really knew or remembered it, I would usually spend the whole day with my family. My uncle, my sister, and even my dad would send me warm wishes, and my mom would cook all of my favorite dishes. It should have been enough, and in many ways, it was. But somehow, I always felt like something was missing.

Honestly, I can't even remember how I felt on my very first birthdays. Was I excited? Did I look forward to them every year? I think my younger self would probably be disappointed to know that now, I barely feel anything when my birthday comes around.

It's not that I'm ungrateful for everything my family has done for me. I've always been happy to receive so much love from them. But even so, I've never really seen this day as something particularly special. To me, my birthday has always felt more or less like any other ordinary day.

But this year marks a huge milestone in my life: I have got myself a lovely girlfriend.

She's creative, energetic, and incredibly talented at drawing. If you're reading this, I want you to know that I truly love your drawings. They're all beautiful in their own way. Somehow, they've brought me back to the days when I used to love drawing too, reminding me of the passion I once had for it. Thank you for that, I love you.

For the first time in years, I found myself genuinely looking forward to my birthday. Not because I expected gifts or celebrations, but because I knew I would get to spend it with someone who has a way of making even ordinary moments feel special.

I felt even happier when my girlfriend insisted on choosing a birthday present for me. She would casually ask about my favorite things, about the little things that made me happy, and she even picked up details from our everyday conversations. Looking back, all of that reminded me of how much thought and care she had put into this day. Sometimes I really wonder if I'm the luckiest guy in the world to be her boyfriend.

On the day itself, we met at our university campus. The weather had been incredibly hot lately, so we went up to the school rooftop to rest, where the breeze made everything much more comfortable. To my surprise, she had even prepared a cake for me: A tiramisu she had ordered from her friend. It was such a thoughtful gesture, and I could tell she genuinely wanted to make my birthday feel complete. It was delicious, too.

After that, she handed me my present. Everything was packed inside a blue plaid paper bag, which immediately made me laugh because it reminded me of the blue plaid shirts I always wear. She once asked me about how obsessed I seem to be with plaid shirts, even though I never really noticed it myself. I just wear whatever I have.

I started taking everything out one by one. The first thing I saw was a light blue pencil case, the biggest item in the bag. I suddenly remembered how, some time ago, she had asked me what I wanted for my birthday, and I had casually mentioned that I needed a new pencil case because my old one was worn out. She actually bought it.

I immediately moved all my things from the old one into the new one, and to my delight, everything fit perfectly, with plenty of space left. Even something as simple as that made me weirdly happy. Before I could reach for the next item, she took it out herself and handed it to me. It was a pencil. A small gift, sure, but I still appreciated it because it felt so thoughtful.

Then came the last item: a small nylon bag containing some letters and something I couldn't identify. She smiled and challenged me to guess what it was, reminding me that she had dropped hints during our previous conversations.

I tried my best to connect all the dots, but honestly, I had no idea.

Defeated, I opened the bag and there it was.

A Snow Miku keychain.

I genuinely couldn't believe my eyes.

I looked at her in shock, and she just smiled before reminding me how she had kept bringing up Snow Miku in our recent conversations. Apparently, all of that had been part of her plan. Looking back, I probably should have suspected something, but I just thought she had suddenly become interested in the topic.

I was ridiculously happy. The fact that she had paid so much attention to something I loved meant more to me than I could properly put into words.

But then she told me there was still one more surprise and pointed at the letter.

When I opened it, another Snow Miku appeared right in front of me. She had drawn it herself and designed it so it would pop up as the letter opened. It was such a creative and unexpected gift that my brain completely stopped working for a moment.

I was overwhelmed with joy.

At that moment, I realized something: this was the first birthday that had ever truly felt special to me.

And if anything, it made me want to do my very best when her birthday comes around, so that I can show her even a fraction of the happiness she gave me today.

After finishing the present-giving section, we continued our lovey-dovey date.

You know, she had recently brought up the fact that I barely ever make a move and that she wanted me to be more proactive. So, since I was the one responsible for planning the date, I had secretly been setting everything up so that by the end of the day, I could finally give her a hug and maybe even a small kiss on the cheek.

On our last date, we had wanted to try an ice cream shop together, but we ended up giving up on that idea because she was sick and couldn't eat ice cream. So today, I decided to treat her to some ice cream instead - not at that shop, but at another one.

And there, she finally showed me her twintails.

This was something I had asked her to show me before, but she hadn't been able to do it back then. Honestly, she looked way too cute. It should probably be illegal.

She even gave me a kiss on my left hand. At that moment, I made the completely rational decision that I would never wash that hand again.

Then, as if that wasn't enough, we proceeded to do so many couple things that we probably ended up feeding everyone around us an unhealthy amount of “dog food.” To all the staff there: we are deeply sorry. Please forgive us.

It was a fun and wonderful experience.

After that, we headed to our final location: a small park under Ba Son Bridge. I had chosen it because, based on all my very serious research, it didn't seem too crowded and therefore felt like the perfect place for our shy-couple hug-and-kiss mission.

The weather was a bit cloudy that day, so we didn't get to see the sunset. But honestly, the scenery was still beautiful. The city lights slowly turning on, the deep blue sky gradually fading into darkness - it all felt strangely peaceful.

I hadn't told her anything about my plan, because I knew she loved surprises.

As we walked, she kept talking about how beautiful the sky was. And every time she said it, she somehow looked even cuter.

Eventually, I couldn't hold it in anymore and told her that I wanted to hug her.

She looked a little surprised, then said, “Let's find somewhere no one can see us.”

And so began our ridiculously difficult quest to locate a spot with absolutely zero people around.

We walked deeper into the park. Fewer and fewer people were around, but somehow there was always someone nearby.

When we reached the end of the park, there were still people there. She randomly picked a place for us to sit, and we stayed there for a while watching people pass by.

Meanwhile, my brain was operating at full capacity:

This spot? Nah. That spot? Also nah. Why are there humans everywhere?

Not wanting to waste too much time overthinking, I asked her if we could keep walking.

We eventually reached a riverside landing where people could stop to enjoy the view. There were still plenty of people there, mostly couples scattered around, but the space was large enough that it didn't feel too crowded.

On the way there, she kissed my right hand.

And just like that, both of my hands had now been kissed.

Great.

Now I couldn't wash either of them.

We stood by a handrail with no one too close to us. The atmosphere was perfect: The wind, the faint smell of the river, the fading light, everything. She kept talking about how nice it felt. And at that point, I couldn't hold back anymore.

I told her directly that I wanted to kiss her.

She looked at me and said she had been wondering the whole time why I hadn't done it already, since the atmosphere was so romantic. I started trying to explain myself, but halfway through, I realized something very important:

Reasoning was useless. Just kiss her already.

So I stepped a little closer and kissed her on the cheek.

Then she slowly turned toward me and kissed my cheek too.

Both kisses were quick.

Too quick.

And in that moment, I shamelessly realized that I wanted more. So I told her exactly that and kissed her again, this time for longer. Her reaction afterward was so unbelievably cute that I couldn't help giving her one more kiss.

At that point, I was beginning to suspect that I might already be addicted to kissing her. (This is probably a serious medical condition.)

As we started heading back, I suddenly remembered something important:

I still hadn't given her that hug.

So I asked her to take another route back with me. It led through a darker, quieter walkway where no one was nearby.

That was finally our moment.

I gave her a little signal, and we wrapped our arms around each other.

It was a long hug.

The kind that makes you quietly wish time would stop for just a little while.

And of course, after the hug, I gave her one last kiss.

We finally went home after that, ending our day together.

I know I'll never forget this birthday.

That's exactly why I wrote all of this down.

And if this day taught me anything, it's that I'm looking forward to making many more memories with the person I love.

This is the end of chapter 1105.
